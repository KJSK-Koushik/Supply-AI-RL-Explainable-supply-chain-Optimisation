One simulated day, in this order

1Deliveries

Stock ordered days ago lands. If the warehouse is full, the extra is refused and written off.

2Customers

Demand is drawn for each product, adjusted for weekday and month.

3Sales

Sell what is in stock. A shortfall is a lost sale with a penalty, carried as backlog.

4Orders

Today’s orders are placed after demand is known, as a real buyer does.

5Profit

sales − purchases − storage − fees − lost-sale penalty − overflow loss.
