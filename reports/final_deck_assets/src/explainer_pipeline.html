The explainer: facts in, checked sentences out

The LLM is never asked why. It only restates what the simulator computed, and every number is checked.
1 Facts

Simulator facts

  • stock, orders on the way
  • days of cover, recent demand
  • what was ordered, from whom
  • supplier delivery time, reliability
➜
2 LLM

Writes 2–3 sentences

  • 3 free models tried in order
  • tries again if a model is busy
  • not allowed to invent reasons
➜
3 Check

Number check

  • every number must appear in the facts
  • all key details must be included
➜
4 Delivered

Shown to the manager

  • pass: the LLM text
  • fail: a fixed template instead

What a delivered explanation looks like

“Product 21212 holds 2,438 units in stock with nothing on order, providing 14.5 days of cover at the typical daily demand of 169 units. Recent demand has averaged 89 units over the last seven days. No order was placed today.”

Measured on 30 real decisions

Raw LLM output: 5 of 30 contained an invented number (83% accurate).
After the check: none of the 5 reached the user (100% correct).
