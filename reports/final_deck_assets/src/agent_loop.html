How the agent learns: one daily loop

Every day the agent sees the shop, picks orders, and is scored on that day’s profit.
Environment

The simulated shop

  • 10 products, 3 suppliers, shared 20,000-unit warehouse
  • Random customers, delays, partial shipments, outages
  • Crises injected from the LLM generator
  • 180 days per episode, first 14 not scored
State →88 numbers: stock, stock on the way, days of cover, recent demand, season, supplier status
← Actionper product: 1 of 21 options
(7 quantity levels × 3 suppliers)
← Rewardthat day’s profit, scaled and clipped
Agent

MaskablePPO

  • Small network, two layers of 256 units, trains on CPU
  • Learning rate 5e-5, 2 million training steps
  • Quantity and supplier chosen together, so training is 2.8× faster
Action masking: offline suppliers and orders too big for the warehouse are blocked before the agent chooses. Worth about 3×.
